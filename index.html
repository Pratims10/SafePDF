Verified Indian Bank, Broker, Tax & Bill Password Rules

SafePDF automatically generates and tests these exact institutional formats in memory when you enter your details in the Universal Keyring.

1. Indian Banks (Savings Accounts & Credit Cards)

HDFC Bank Savings: Customer ID
Credit Card: First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + DDMM (or Last 4 digits of Card)
ICICI Bank Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (lowercase) + DDMM (e.g. prat1408) or Customer ID
State Bank of India (SBI) Savings: Last 5 digits of Mobile + DDMMYY (or DDMMYYYY@Last4Mob)
SBI Card: DDMMYYYY + Last 4 digits of Card
Axis Bank Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + DDMM (or DDYY), or 9-digit Customer ID
Kotak Mahindra Bank Savings & Card: CRN (Customer Relationship No.), DDMMYYYY, or First 4 letters (lowercase) + DDMM
IDFC FIRST Bank Savings & Card: Date of Birth in DDMM (4 digits) or DDMMYYYY (8 digits)
IndusInd Bank Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (lowercase) + DDMM (e.g. prat1408) or DDMMYYYY
Bank of Baroda (BoB) Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (lowercase) + DDMM (e.g. prat1408)
Punjab National Bank (PNB) Savings: 16-digit Account Number or Customer ID
Credit Card: DDMMYYYY
Yes Bank Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + DDMM (e.g. PRAT1408) or Customer ID
Canara Bank & Union Bank Savings: Customer ID or First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + DDMM (or Last 4 digits of Account)
RBL, Federal & AU Bank Savings & Card: First 4 letters of Name (CAPS/lowercase) + DDMM or DDMMYYYY
Standard Chartered & HSBC Statements: Date of Birth DDMMYY or First 4 letters of Name + DDMM
American Express (Amex) Credit Card: First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + DDMM or DDMMYYYY
Bank of India & Indian Bank Savings: Customer ID or First 4 letters of Name (lowercase) + DDMM

2. Stock Brokers, Demat & Mutual Fund Statements

Zerodha (Console / Contract Notes) 10-character PAN in UPPERCASE (e.g. ABCDE1234F)
Groww (Tax P&L / Demat) 10-character PAN in UPPERCASE (ABCDE1234F) or DDMMYYYY
Angel One & Upstox 10-character PAN in UPPERCASE (ABCDE1234F)
INDmoney & Dhan 10-character PAN in UPPERCASE or Date of Birth (DDMMYYYY)
NSDL & CDSL CAS 10-character PAN in UPPERCASE (ABCDE1234F)
CAMS / KFintech Mailback 10-character PAN (CAPS/lowercase) or user-defined portal key

3. Income Tax, Salary & Government Portals

AIS / TIS & ITR-V Acknowledgement 10-char PAN (lowercase) + DOB DDMMYYYY (e.g. abcde1234f14081995)
Form 16 (Part A & Part B) First 5 chars of PAN (CAPS) + DDMMYYYY, or Full PAN + DDMMYYYY
Form 26AS (TRACES) Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format (e.g. 14081995)
e-PAN Card PDF (NSDL / UTI) Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format (e.g. 14081995)
UIDAI e-ID PDF First 4 letters of Name (CAPS) + Year of Birth YYYY (e.g. PRAT1995)
Airtel, Jio & Vi Postpaid Bills First 4 letters of Name (lowercase) + Last 4 digits of Mobile No. or DDMM
